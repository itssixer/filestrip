# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place anyone can contribute without harassment, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or expression.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind. Technical disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
- Review code, not people.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith and lacking context, not acting in bad faith.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that expands the scope or the dependency surface.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/itssixer/filestrip/security/advisories/new) or a direct message to a maintainer. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block accounts. Decisions aim to protect contributors, not to punish.
